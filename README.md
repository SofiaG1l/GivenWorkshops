## Programming and Data Analysis in R

### R for Social Scientists

I designed this course to give social scientists all that is necessary to start 
using R in their everyday work. It is designed for academics in different 
career stages (from PhD students to professors) and with different backgrounds. 
It does not matter if they are qualitative researchers that have never used R, or 
quantitative researchers that want to learn new topics or move out of SPSS/STATA. 

The course has received very good reviews. Furthermore, some departments at 
VU have requested that I teach it to their staff, to help speed-up the department 
transition to fully using R. 

The course can be accessed here:
[https://github.com/SofiaG1l/R_Course](https://github.com/SofiaG1l/R_Course)

It can be cited this way: 
Gil-Clavel, S. (2025). R-Course: R for Social Scientists (Version 1.0). 
[https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17184812](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17184812). (Original work published 2019)


### R Tools for Teaching and Research

A common problem when teaching using R is that there is a lot  
of friction between achieving the course intended learning outcomes and the amount of time 
lecturers have to spend solving students’ R installations.

This course is designed to give social scientists all that is necessary to ease 
their teaching and research endeavors using R. In it, academics learn how to:

* Standardize students’ homework. So that, it is easier to discover fraud and to grade!
* Make the best use of VU resources to teach R: JupyterHub and AmCAT.
* Publish their course materials with a DOI, while performing version control using GIT.

The course can be accessed here:

  [https://github.com/SofiaG1l/R_Course_Tools](https://github.com/SofiaG1l/R_Course_Tools).

It can be cited this way: 

  Gil-Clavel, S. (2026). R-Course: Tools for teaching and research. (Version v.1.1) [Computer software]. Zenodo. [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22036755](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22036755).


## Workshops on Content Analysis of Digital Data

These are 3-hours workshops that aim to give a general overview on the 
use of content analysis using digital data. They have a focus on population research. 
The data is retrieved using application programming interfaces from diverse platforms. 

The course has been followed by bachelor, master, and doctoral students, as well as 
professors from different international institutions, and it has received very 
good reviews.

### Content Analysis

The workshop gives participants a brief, but holistic overview of the basics of manual and
automatic content analysis. For this, we retrieved, annotated, and analyzed data 
from the Guardian using AI agents.

You can access all the material [here](https://github.com/SofiaG1l/CAnD3_2026).

Originally, this workshop was requested and funded by [CAnD3](https://www.mcgill.ca/cand3/) as part of 
their professional development program [May 2026].

### Retrieving and Analyzing the Guardian News

During this workshop, participants coded a full pipeline to analyse text as data.
The first half focused on retrieving and cleaning the data. The second half, 
focused on analyzing text as data. 

You can access all the material [here](https://github.com/SofiaG1l/Bytes_n_Bites_SGC).

Originally, this workshop was requested and funded by [Bytes & Bites](https://libcal.vu.nl/event/4521460),
Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, as part of their professional development program [Apr. 2026].

### Retrieving and Analyzing data from Facebook and Twitter

The goal of this workshop is to provide a general introduction to what an API is and 
how to use the Facebook and Twitter APIs. In this course, participants learned how to query 
and download data on the estimated number of Facebook users who match the demographic 
characteristics they were interested in analyzing. They also learned how to download 
tweets that match specific text searchs.

You can access all the material [here](https://github.com/SofiaG1l/Taller_COLMEX_API).

Originally, this workshop was requested and funded by [El Colegio de Mexico](https://cedua.colmex.mx/evento/v-coloquio-internacional-estudiantil-en-poblacion-ciudad-y-ambiente/inicio) and 
[CIMAT](https://www.cimat.mx/coloquios-del-cimat/) as part of their professional 
development programs [Sep. 2022 & Apr. 2023].

### Using the Facebook Marketing API

The aim of this course is to give a quick overview on what it is and how to use 
the Facebook Marketing API. In this course, you will learn how to query and retrieve
aggregated data of different users' demographic characteristics.

You can access all the material [here](https://github.com/SofiaG1l/Using_Facebook_API), 
and check the video of the course [here](https://iussp.org/en/iussp-seminar-digital-demography-era-big-data).

Originally, this workshop was requested by IUSSP and funded by BBVA Foundation [link](https://iussp.org/en/iussp-seminar-digital-demography-era-big-data)
as part of their professional development program [Jun. 2019].
